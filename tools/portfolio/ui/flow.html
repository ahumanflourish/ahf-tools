          Read this alongside it
          • This is not investment advice. It makes no recommendation and says nothing about what you should do next.
          • Every figure comes out of the engine, and every flagged item comes out of what Claude actually returned. Nothing here is written by hand.
          • A reference strategy is a yardstick, not a menu. Nothing here says that fund was available to you, in your plan or at that price.
          • The result is historical and benchmark-dependent. A different reference, or a different period, gives a different answer.
          • A short period is a small sample. Five years containing one bear market is thin evidence about anything.
          • Returns aren’t the whole job. A fee may also buy tax work, planning, or the coaching that stopped you selling at the bottom. This measures none of that.
          • Nothing is stored on this device unless you download it. Reading your documents sends them to Claude on your own account, and is billed there.
