Nothing reaches the maths until you press “looks right”, and every figure after that comes out of the engine.

        Read this alongside it

        • The review table is the only way in. Typing, pasting and AI extraction all land there, and none of them can write past it.
        • The checks on the table are deterministic and run before the engine, on every keystroke. The arithmetic does not: analyse() runs when you press the gate, and again only if you change the reference the capture bar is measured against.
        • Nothing is silently dropped. A line that could not be read is shown verbatim with the reason; a row treated as a transfer is struck through and kept, never deleted.
        • This is not investment advice. It makes no recommendation and says nothing about what you should do next.
        • A reference strategy is a yardstick, not a menu. Nothing here says that fund was available to you, in your plan or at that price.
        • The result is historical and benchmark-dependent. A different reference, or a different period, gives a different answer — switch the reference and watch every figure move.
        • A short period is a small sample. Five years containing one bear market is thin evidence about anything.
        • Returns aren’t the whole job. A fee may also buy tax work, planning, or the coaching that stopped you selling at the bottom. This measures none of that.
        • Export the JSON if you want to keep this. There is no autosave and nothing is stored on this device unless you ask for it.
